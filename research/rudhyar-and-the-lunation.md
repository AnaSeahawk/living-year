# Rudhyar and the lunation

## What is taken from him

The eight phases used in the journal (New, Crescent, First quarter, Gibbous, Full, Disseminating, Last quarter, Balsamic) come from Dane Rudhyar's *The Lunation Cycle* (1967). The ranges used in the notation are New 1º–45º, Crescent 46º–90º, First quarter 91º–135º, Gibbous 136º–180º, Full 181º–225º, Disseminating 226º–270º, Last quarter 271º–315º, Balsamic 316º–360º. They are the Moon's distance from the Sun in 45º steps.

## What is not taken from him

Naming that distance with the twelve signs, so that a new Moon is 1º ♈ of the lunation, is Ana and her husband's own extension. No source was found for it. It is not attributed to Rudhyar or anyone else. Who first did this after Rudhyar is unknown.

## The unchecked aside

Laying the Moon's 213º from the Ascendant of Ana's chart (3°17′ ♐) gives about 6º ♋. Rudhyar's tradition calls that the Part of Fortune. This is an unchecked sum, and the claim about the tradition has not been verified.
