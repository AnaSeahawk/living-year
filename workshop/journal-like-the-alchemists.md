# The Living Year: Journal Like the Alchemists

**Draft proposal.** Not confirmed by Ana.

The workshop is complete on its own. It is also a way to feel what the membership would be like. Each person works in [the First Notebook](../notebook/first-notebook.md).

## Format

- One to one: 90 minutes, no discussion period added. Booked online (the 90-minute session, US$90, at cal.com/anaseahawk/90minutes).
- Group: arranged with Ana directly. Two hours: 90 minutes plus half an hour of group discussion.

## Outline (90 minutes)

| Minutes | Part | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | The watch and the Sun | Welcome. "The Sun tells the time of day. The watch tells when to catch the train." |
| 10–25 | The year as a circle | 360º from 1º ♈, four quarters, the fourfold work |
| 25–40 | The Moon | The phases. The Moon's distance from the Sun |
| 40–55 | Where you are today | Each person finds today's Sun, Moon and phase, and their solar noon against clock noon |
| 55–70 | Where you began | Birth degree, birth Moon, solar return |
| 70–85 | The first entry | Everyone writes one |
| 85–90 | Where next | The Living Year. No dates promised |
